Welcome to Simpson Productions

Founded in 2019, Simpson Productions is an interdiscipinary, collaborative partnership between the departments of Theatre and Music at Simpson College. Previously, two separate production entities, this merger of theatrical and operatic programming fosters greater creativity and connectivity across the college, allowing us to share resources, experiences, and talents of students and professors alike. We seek to offer innovative and diverse programing to both the Simpson community and the greater Central Iowa region, keeping our college's values of curiosity, discovery, and integrity at the core.

Vision:

To create a unique, nationally recognized academic performance community for the study and practice of theatre, musicals, and opera.

Mission:

  • To present high-quality productions that offer our students and audiences experiences that embrace diverse performance modes.
  • To explore performance as a communication tool and form of service that can reach multiple audiences.
  • To provide students with various opportunities to design, perform, and create collaboratively.
