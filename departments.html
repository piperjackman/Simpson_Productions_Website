Production Departments

Scenic & Props

The Scenic and Props departments design, build, source, and maintain all set pieces, hand props, and scenic elements for productions. This includes carpentry, painting, crafting, and creative problem‑solving to bring each world to life.

Lighting & Sound

The Lighting and Sound departments shape the atmosphere of each production through visibility, mood, audio playback, microphones, reinforcement, and sound design. These teams collaborate closely to support storytelling through technical artistry.

Costume & Makeup

The Costume and Makeup department designs, builds, and maintains all garments, wigs, hair, and makeup looks for productions. They create character‑driven designs and ensure performers are stage‑ready for every performance.

Marketing

The Marketing department promotes productions through graphic design, social media, photography, videography, and campus outreach. They help connect Simpson Productions with the wider community.

Front of House

The Front of House team manages the audience experience, including ticketing, ushering, accessibility support, and lobby operations. They ensure every patron feels welcomed and informed.

Stage Management

Stage Management oversees communication, scheduling, rehearsal processes, and backstage coordination. They ensure each production runs smoothly from the first rehearsal to the final curtain.
